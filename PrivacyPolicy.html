Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

Learn how Sri Takshashila Gurukul collects, uses, protects, and manages your personal information.

Last updated: 23 June 2026

Sri Takshashila Gurukul respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, participate in our programmes, volunteer, or make a donation.

By using this website or sharing information with us, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information when you interact with Sri Takshashila Gurukul through our website, forms, donation pages, events, campaigns, programmes, or communication channels.

Personal Information

Personal information may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Residential or mailing address
  • Age or date of birth, where required
  • Donation and transaction details
  • Volunteer or programme registration details
  • Identity or eligibility documents where legally or operationally required
  • Any information voluntarily shared through forms, emails, calls, or messages

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on the website
  • Referral source
  • General location information

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries, requests, and feedback
  • Process donations and issue acknowledgements or receipts
  • Register beneficiaries, volunteers, participants, or supporters
  • Manage educational, awareness, scholarship, and community programmes
  • Send programme updates, newsletters, event invitations, or impact reports
  • Maintain donor and supporter records
  • Improve our website, services, campaigns, and outreach activities
  • Comply with legal, financial, auditing, or regulatory requirements
  • Prevent fraudulent, unauthorised, or unlawful activity
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of our organisation and community

3. Donations and Payment Information

When you make a donation, your payment may be processed through a third-party payment gateway, bank, UPI service, or other payment provider.

Sri Takshashila Gurukul does not generally store complete credit card, debit card, UPI PIN, CVV, or net banking credentials on its website. Payment providers process such information according to their own privacy and security policies.

We may retain donor details, donation amount, transaction reference, payment date, and other records required for accounting, auditing, tax, receipt generation, and regulatory compliance.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies, analytics tools, and similar technologies to understand website usage, improve performance, and measure the effectiveness of campaigns.

We may use services such as Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager. These tools may collect information about your device, browser, website activity, and general location.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect certain features or the overall functionality of the website.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share information only where reasonably necessary with:

  • Authorised staff members, trustees, volunteers, or programme coordinators
  • Payment gateways, banks, and transaction service providers
  • Website hosting, email, analytics, IT, and communication service providers
  • Auditors, accountants, consultants, or professional advisers
  • Government authorities or regulatory bodies where required by law
  • Partner organisations involved in approved programmes or initiatives

These parties are expected to use the information only for the purpose for which it was shared and to maintain appropriate confidentiality and security.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no website, internet transmission, storage system, or digital communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, while we strive to protect your information, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, maintain records, and enforce agreements.

Donation, beneficiary, tax, and financial records may be retained for a longer period where required by applicable laws or regulations.

8. Information Relating to Children

As an organisation involved in education and community development, Sri Takshashila Gurukul may collect limited information relating to children or students for scholarships, educational support, events, mentoring, or programme participation.

Where required, such information will be collected with the consent of a parent, guardian, school, or authorised representative.

We take additional care to protect information relating to minors and do not knowingly use such information for unrelated marketing purposes.

9. Photographs, Videos, and Impact Stories

During programmes, events, campaigns, or community initiatives, photographs, videos, testimonials, and impact stories may be collected for awareness, reporting, fundraising, documentation, and promotional purposes.

Where appropriate, we seek consent from participants, parents, guardians, beneficiaries, or authorised representatives before publishing identifiable information.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, social media platforms, payment gateways, or partner websites.

Sri Takshashila Gurukul is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Withdraw consent for certain communications
  • Request deletion of information where legally permitted
  • Opt out of newsletters, updates, or promotional communication
  • Raise concerns regarding the use of your information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details provided below.

12. Email and Communication Preferences

We may send emails, messages, programme updates, newsletters, donation acknowledgements, campaign information, or event invitations where you have provided your contact details.

You may unsubscribe from non-essential communication at any time by using the unsubscribe option, replying to the message, or contacting us directly.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Sri Takshashila Gurukul may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, programmes, technology, or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page along with the revised “Last updated” date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
